One thing before we start

This app talks to Google Gemini to read your photo and write the diagnosis. It needs your own free API key. It is stored on this phone only and sent to nobody but Google.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey
  2. Sign in and press Create API key
  3. Copy it and paste it below

The free tier is far more than one person can use. On the free tier Google may use the data to improve their products; a paid key does not.
